# 2.9 – Adaptive calorie goal from weight trend

Issue: jjerner/FitTracker#30 (effort L, `needs-decision`). HANDOVER.md → Proposed roadmap, row 2.9: "adaptive calorie goal from weight trend + intake (MacroFactor's main idea). We already have both data series." Listed with 5.2 under "Bigger bets to discuss".

## Goal

Work out how many calories the user really burns per day (estimated maintenance, or TDEE) from what they ate and how their weight trend moved. Show it, and let them tap once to use it as their calorie goal.

## The idea in one line

If you ate 2 300 kcal a day for 4 weeks and your trend weight stayed flat, you burn about 2 300. If the trend dropped 1 kg, you burned about 7 700 kcal more than you ate, which is 275 more per day, so about 2 575.

`maintenance ≈ average daily intake − (trend change in kg × 7 700) / days between`

## Slice 1 in detail

### Calculation (`estimateMaintenance`)

- Window: the last 28 days before today (today is left out, it is still being logged, same rule as `NutritionAveragesCard`).
- Weight: run `weightTrend` on all fetched weigh-ins (so the first point in the window still has a full week behind it). Take the trend value at the first and the last weigh-in inside the window. `daysBetween` = days between those two dates.
- Intake: average `caloriesKcal` over the `daily_nutrition` rows in the window. Days with nothing logged are skipped (the view leaves them out already).
- Returns `null` (not enough data) when: fewer than the minimum logged food days, or `daysBetween` is below the minimum (see open question 3 for both numbers).
- Returns `{ kcal, loggedDays, daysBetween, trendChangeKg }`, `kcal` rounded to the nearest 10.
- 7 700 kcal per kg is a constant with a one-line comment.

### Calories → macros (`goalsWithCalories`)

The goals screen works out calories from the macros (`protein × 4 + carbs × 4 + fat × 9`, `profile/goals.tsx:60-63`). So setting a calorie goal has to change the macros too. Pure helper `goalsWithCalories(current: NutritionGoals, kcal: number): NutritionGoals`. Which macros move is open question 2. Whatever is picked, the result must satisfy `caloriesKcal === round(4P + 4C + 9F)` so the goals screen shows the same number after a save. No macro goes below 0.

### Card on Progress

New `MaintenanceCard` in `src/app/(tabs)/progress.tsx`, placed right after `WeightCard`:

```
Estimated maintenance
≈ 2 350 kcal / day
From 22 logged days and your weight trend (−0.6 kg) over the last 4 weeks.
Your goal: 2 000 kcal
[ Use 2 350 kcal as my goal ]
```

- Not enough data: one muted line, for example "Log your food on at least N days and weigh in at least once a week for 2 weeks to see this."
- No goal saved yet: hide the "Your goal" line. The button still works (it starts from the `DEFAULT_GOALS` macros in `profile/goals.tsx`, moved or exported so both screens use the same values).
- Button saves with `saveGoals(goalsWithCalories(goals, kcal))`, shows a short "Goal updated" line on success and an error line on failure. Hidden when the goal already equals the suggestion.
- No `Alert.alert` confirm dialog: buttons in `Alert` do nothing on web, so the flow could not be tested there. The goal can always be changed back on the goals screen (but see open question 5).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] With enough data, Progress shows "Estimated maintenance ≈ X kcal / day", the number of logged days, the trend change in kg, and the current calorie goal.
- [ ] X matches the formula: flat trend → X equals average intake; trend down 1 kg over 28 days on 2 000 kcal/day → X = 2 280 (2 000 + 7 700 / 28 ≈ 275, rounded to 10).
- [ ] Today's food is not counted. Days with nothing logged are not counted as 0.
- [ ] With too little data, the card shows the "not enough data" line and no number or button.
- [ ] Tapping "Use X kcal as my goal" saves it. Home ring, Food tab ring and the goals screen then show X (the goals screen's calculated calories equal X).
- [ ] The button is hidden when the goal already equals X. A failed save shows an error and leaves the goal unchanged.
- [ ] No SQL, no new dependency, no native change.
- [ ] `npx tsc --noEmit` and `npx expo lint` are clean; jest passes.

## Open questions

1. Slice 1 suggestion: show only maintenance (button sets goal = maintenance), or add an unsaved lose / keep / gain picker in the card (for example −0.5 / 0 / +0.25 kg per week) so the button sets maintenance ± pace? Saving the pace is slice 2 either way.
2. When the calorie goal changes, which macros move? (a) keep protein and fat grams, carbs take the whole difference; (b) keep protein, split the difference between carbs and fat in today's ratio; (c) scale all three by the same factor.
3. Minimum data before showing a number: how many logged food days in the 28-day window (for example 14), and how far apart the first and last weigh-in must be (for example 14 days)? And is 28 days the right window?
4. Half-logged days (a forgotten dinner) make intake look low, so the estimate comes out too low. Accept that for slice 1 and say so in the card text, or leave out days far below the user's average (a guess that can be wrong for real fasting days)?
5. Apply flow: is one tap with no confirm OK (undo = edit goals by hand), or should the button open the goals screen pre-filled so the user presses Save there?
6. Order with 5.2 and 5.3: build 2.9 slice 1 first, or 5.2 first (it gives every new user a starting goal and the lose/keep/gain choice that 2.9 slice 2 needs)? If 5.3 (training vs rest day goals) ships first, which goal does "Use as my goal" change?
7. Where does the card live: Progress (next to the weight chart, proposed), the goals screen, or both?

## Out of scope

- Saving goal direction or pace, weekly check-ins, auto-apply (slices 2 and 3).
- Smoothing, a maintenance-over-time chart, outlier handling (slice 4).
- Height / age / sex formulas (that is 5.2) and any new Profile fields.

- Exercise or step calories, wearables, Withings.

## Decisions (PM, 2026-10-19)

These override slice 1's proposal and open questions above. Ticket: jjerner/FitTracker#54 (GOALS-2, step 2 of 6 of #43). Slices 2–4 are tracked as #55 (save pace), #57 (weekly check-in), #58 (better estimate); rest-day goals are #56.

**Build after #53 (goal helper)**: this reuses its pace list, per-goal macro defaults, `macrosFor()` and the Goals screen's % / g/kg display.

1. **Card shows maintenance ± pace:** the same pace chips as the helper (Lose 0.75 / 0.5 / 0.25, Keep, Gain 0.25 kg per week), default **Keep**, not saved (saving is #55). Suggested kcal = maintenance + pace × 7 700 / 7, with the helper's floor if sex is known (else 1 200).
2. **Apply flow:** the button "Use X kcal as my goal" opens the Goals screen **pre-filled** (route params: kcal and goal direction). Nothing is saved until the user taps Save Goals. This replaces "save in one tap" and the "Goal updated" line. `goalsWithCalories` is replaced by:
   - **Suggested split (default):** protein and fat from the per-goal g/kg defaults (`macrosFor`, weight from the trend), carbs = what's left.
   - **Scale my current split:** a chip that scales the current protein, carbs and fat by the same factor (new kcal ÷ current kcal).
   - Each macro shows grams, % of kcal and g/kg (from #53).
3. **Minimum data:** window = the 28 days before today (today left out). Needs **≥ 14 counted food days** and the first and last weigh-in in the window **≥ 14 days apart**. Otherwise: "Keep logging food and weight to see this."
4. **Incomplete days:** skip any day whose kcal is below **half of the median** logged day in the window. Skipped days do not count toward the 14. The card says "N days skipped (looked incomplete)" when N > 0. Unit-test it.
5. **Place:** Progress, right under the weight chart.
6. Rest-day goals (#56) decide later which goal set this card changes; for now there is one goal.
